Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data

While using Our Service, We ask You to provide Us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify You, including:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Date of birth
  • Gender
  • An emergency contact's name, phone number, and relationship to You (optional, provided by You)

Health Data

Because HealthNet is a healthcare appointment and records application, We also collect and store health information necessary to provide the Service, including:

  • Blood group and known allergies
  • Diagnoses, prescriptions, and doctor's notes from visits booked through the Service
  • Vaccination records
  • Lab and diagnostic reports and results
  • Appointment and visit history with doctors and hospitals on the platform

This health information is only ever visible to You and the doctors and hospital staff directly involved in Your care.

Family Member (Dependent) Data

If You add a family member, such as a child or parent, to Your account to manage their appointments and records, We collect the same categories of personal and health data listed above for that dependent. This information is entered and managed by You, the account holder — a dependent added this way does not create or access their own separate account.

Photos and Documents

With Your permission, the Service lets You take or upload a photo for Your profile picture, and photograph or upload medical documents — lab reports, prescriptions, scans/imaging, discharge summaries, and vaccination certificates — to attach to Your records.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect Your Device's precise location, and We do not access Your phone's contacts/address book. We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or any other third-party analytics or advertising SDK.

Use of Your Personal Data

The Company may use Personal Data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain Our Service, including Your account, appointment booking, medical records, prescriptions, and reminders.
  • To deliver Your care, by sharing relevant health information with the specific doctors and hospital staff You book an appointment with.
  • To contact You, by sending one-time passwords (OTP) for login, registration, and password reset, and appointment confirmations and reminders. We do not send marketing communications.
  • To manage Your requests: to attend and manage requests You submit to Us.
  • For business transfers: We may use Your Personal Data to evaluate or conduct a merger, divestiture, restructuring, reorganization, dissolution, or other sale or transfer of some or all of Our assets.

We may share Your Personal Data in the following situations:

  • With the hospitals and doctors You book with, so they can provide medical care.
  • With Service Providers who host or operate the Service's infrastructure, such as cloud hosting and email delivery.
  • For business transfers, as described above, or with Your consent.

We do not sell Your Personal Data, and We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Retention of Your Personal Data

The Company will retain Your Personal Data, including medical records, only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. We will retain and use Your Personal Data for as long as Your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward to comply with applicable healthcare record-keeping and legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce Our legal agreements and policies.

Your information, including Personal Data, is processed at the Company's operating offices and in any other places where the parties involved in the processing are located. This means that this information may be transferred to, and maintained on, computers located outside of Your state, province, country, or other governmental jurisdiction.

The Company will take all steps reasonably necessary to ensure that Your data is treated securely and in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Children's and Minors' Privacy

The Service itself is not directed to children, and We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to independently create their own account. A parent or legal guardian who holds a verified HealthNet Account may add a child as a family member (dependent) on their own Account, in order to book appointments and manage that child's health records. In this case, the child never creates, accesses, or logs into their own account; all data is entered and controlled by the verified adult Account holder.

If You believe a child has been given independent access to the Service in a way that violates this Policy, please contact Us.
